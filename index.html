Key Catalysts & Upcoming Events

Events that could move the copper market

2026-Q1 supply bullish
Grasberg Block Cave Phased Restart
Freeport-McMoRan's Grasberg Block Cave begins phased restart after the Sept 2025 wet material ingress. Full ramp-up extends through 2027 — any further delays would tighten global supply further.
2026-Q1 demand bullish
AI Data Center Copper Demand Surge
Goldman Sachs estimates ~475 kmt of copper demand from data center installations in 2026, up ~110 kmt year-over-year. AI infrastructure buildout continues to accelerate.
2026-Q1 policy bullish
US Section 232 Tariff Decision on Refined Copper
Potential US tariffs on refined copper imports under Section 232 investigation. US copper already trades at a premium to LME — tariffs would further tighten domestic supply.
2026-Q2 supply bullish
Chinese Smelter Raw Material Shortage
J.P. Morgan flags potential reversal in Chinese refined copper production growth as smelters face raw material shortages. China's refined output growth expected to halve to ~5%.
2026-H1 supply neutral
Anglo Teck Merger Completion
Teck Resources and Anglo American merger of equals expected to complete within 12-18 months. Combined entity would be a top-tier copper producer focused on critical minerals.

Market Context: World Bureau of Metal Statistics, LME, Shanghai Futures Exchange.
